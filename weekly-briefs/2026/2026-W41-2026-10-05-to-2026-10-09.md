## 1. The Week in One Paragraph

The long-end sell-off that Monday's editions filed under Fed mechanics and a French carry-trade unwind (WSJ, Mon 10/5; FT, Mon 10/5) had become, by Thursday, a simultaneous fiscal-credibility repricing across the US, UK, France and Japan, with the FT printing the US 30-year at 6.04%, a 24-year high (FT, Thu 10/8), against 5.62% on Monday (FT, Mon 10/5). The transmission channel also moved: from rates into credit (CCC spreads at 12 points, widest since 2022, FT, Wed 10/7; Paramount paper marked down, WSJ, Mon 10/5 and FT, Wed 10/7) and into the AI-financing chain itself (Anthropic's $60bn Broadcom-backed lease financing with the $18bn junior tranche held back, FT, Wed 10/7; SpaceX's $40bn Nvidia purchase via junk bonds and private credit, FT, Thu 10/8). Equity indices ran to records through Wednesday on four stocks (S&P 500 7,722.72 on Mon to 7,818.93–7,828.84 on Wed; FT, Mon 10/5, WSJ and FT, Wed 10/7) and fell together on Thursday (FT, Thu 10/8), with the forward P/E down from 22.2x to 19.3x since January (WSJ, Thu 10/8). What is true now that Monday's papers did not show: the "AI benefits are essentially infinite" equity bet (WSJ, Tue 10/6) is being funded by the same credit market that is repricing duration, and the sovereign "safe anchor" prior has failed in France and is failing in the US.

## 6. Geopolitics & Policy Arc

**Dominant thread: Iran / the Gulf, reaching Europe's bases.** It was the most-repeated hotspot tag (Hormuz/Iran: WSJ, Mon 10/5; Middle East: WSJ, Tue 10/6; Hormuz/Gulf: FT, Wed 10/7; UK basing: WSJ, Thu 10/8).

- Mon: Hormuz exports back near prewar but seven attacks in a week and a US official saying Iran's targeting has "improved"; Washington backed off striking Iranian tankers after a missile barrage on a US base in Jordan; all B-1s pulled from RAF Fairford after a foiled IRGC-linked plot (WSJ, Mon 10/5).
- Tue: the Fairford plot assessed as IRGC-ordered, a second UK vector after the arrests near the base (WSJ, Tue 10/6); Houthis hitting Riyadh airport and an Aramco refinery (WSJ, Tue 10/6).
- Wed: the calculus shifted to shipping capacity (Vitol, $1.3mn/day, $200-oil warning) and to a new security architecture: the Mecca pact between Saudi Arabia, Pakistan and Turkey, triggered for the first time though Turkey's parliament has not ratified it (FT, Wed 10/7).
- Thu: the Fairford withdrawal reframed as an asymmetric-basing vulnerability: a crude plot caused the hurried exit of ~12 of the ~45 B-1s, some leaving before refuelling was arranged (WSJ, Thu 10/8). Houthis struck Riyadh and Aden; the US is reported weighing cuts to its European footprint (FT, Thu 10/8; WSJ, Thu 10/8).

## 10. Steel-Man of the Week

**Consensus view:** AI-driven earnings growth justifies record equity levels, so rising yields can be absorbed (WSJ, Tue 10/6; FT, Wed 10/7; WSJ, Thu 10/8).
