**The AI trade is bifurcating into platform winners and leveraged infrastructure losers, and the market is pricing the split correctly.**

The WSJ markets desk frames this as deal mechanics (all-stock, modest premium, antitrust risk); the more consequential frame is sovereign-dependency risk.

## 12. One To Watch

PHP2Shell — the critical vulnerability disclosed today in PHP, the technology underpinning up to 70% of the world's websites — deserves more attention than its single A1/A6 placement suggests. This is a genuine systemic-infrastructure event with direct relevance to any desk running counterparty exposure to fintechs, e-commerce platforms, or any unpatched PHP-dependent business; the race between AI-accelerated patching and AI-accelerated exploitation is now a standing feature of internet infrastructure risk, not a one-off story, and today's edition treats it as a single news item rather than the opening chapter of a recurring exposure.
