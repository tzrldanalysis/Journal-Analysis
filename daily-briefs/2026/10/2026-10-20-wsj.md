## 3. Geopolitics & Policy

Mechanism: sanctions waiver + new import arrangement, effective immediately, no stated expiration; retaliation risk is more diplomatic than economic (Zelensky says Kyiv again got no early warning of a Russian strike from partners who previously shared intelligence — a direct, measurable degradation of alliance information-sharing). Market implication: U.S. diesel futures down 6.5% from their record. Geopolitical signal: Washington is now actively easing pressure on Moscow while publicly floating a Trump-Putin-Xi trilateral on denuclearization (likely in China, tied to Trump's upcoming APEC visit) — a structural shift in U.S. posture toward Russia that undercuts the sanctions regime Congress built.

**Nuclear arms control vacuum.** New START expired in February with no replacement and no trilateral talks with China yet agreed — this is the most under-discussed structural risk in the paper: a three-way nuclear competition (U.S./Russia/China) with zero legally binding limits, mentioned almost in passing inside the Trump-meeting story.

**Saudi Arabia / Houthi escalation.** The Houthis hit Riyadh's international airport with 20 projectiles (10 drones, 4 ballistic missiles, 4 cruise missiles, 2 unidentified) across at least four strikes in two weeks, killing 13+ in one strike and 300+ injured across the campaign; separately, 53 Yemeni government soldiers were killed in a single ballistic-missile strike on a military camp — among the deadliest attacks since the 2022 ceasefire collapsed this summer. **France: institutional fracture with market spillover.** This is covered in depth in Section 9, but the geopolitical signal is that Le Pen's National Rally — not the government — is positioned as the stabilizing force in voters' eyes amid the worst rioting in years, a direct inversion of National Rally's historical "risky outsider" branding. **Israel: U.S. alliance management under strain.** VP Vance explicitly declined to endorse Netanyahu ahead of the Oct. 27 election — a public break from Trump, who called Netanyahu a "Great Warrior Prime Minister." Structural read: Vance is positioning the U.S.-Israel relationship as one alliance among several rather than uniquely special, a rhetorical shift that Republicans distinguishing themselves from traditional pro-Israel orthodoxy will have to navigate as Vance becomes the likely 2028 GOP standard-bearer.

**India.** The "Cockroach Party" youth protests have already forced partial reversal of a 130-million-name voter-roll purge and claimed a ministerial resignation (education minister, July). With Uttar Pradesh's local elections approaching and BJP having already lost its 2024 parliamentary majority there, Modi's government — long seen as unassailable on security/dissent — is "scared of young people," per a Brown University political scientist quoted in the piece.
