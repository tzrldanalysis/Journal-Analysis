## 9. Europe Watch

France is at the center of today's European story. The ECB's Thursday meeting was overshadowed by Lagarde's announcement that she will step down in February, three months before her term's scheduled end — and France's 10-year government bond yield has jumped to 5%, its highest level since 2002, in a "big selloff" that is "rocking" French bond markets.
