## 5. Steel-Man

The dominant narrative in today's coverage is "the AI bubble is cracking" — SoftBank's writedown, Anthropic's IPO repricing, and CoreWeave's share-price weakness are being read as confirmation. The steel-man case against that narrative, using only today's evidence: the demand and backlog data embedded in the same reporting actually came in ahead of what the market had been bracing for — the weakness is concentrated in financing and valuation multiples, not in underlying usage or contracted demand. A capital-structure repricing at the most leveraged, most recently public names (SoftBank's financing vehicles, CoreWeave) is not the same claim as a demand-side bubble bursting, and today's edition conflates the two more than the underlying facts support.
