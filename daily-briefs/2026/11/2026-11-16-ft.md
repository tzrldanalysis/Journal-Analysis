## 6. Prior Under Pressure

**Prior:** Concentrated "long America" positioning — by US households (>40% of the US market), foreign retail investors (~20% and rising), and nearly every national cohort described in Ruchir Sharma's column — is a durable, low-risk default allocation because US markets simply outperform.

Today's tape is a live stress-test of exactly this: a hawkish Fed repricing (10yr to 5.13%), a concentrated AI-sector equity reversal, and gold *and* crypto falling simultaneously (not a flight to safety but a liquidity-driven unwind) are happening in the single most crowded, single-market-concentrated positioning regime markets have ever recorded. The prior that this concentration is "safe because it's America" is precisely what today's price action — a US-specific tech/AI decline that foreign indices are not fully sharing — calls into question.
