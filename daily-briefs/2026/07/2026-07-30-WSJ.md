## 5. Steel-Man

**Steel-man:** The 2-year yield actually fell on the announcement — if this were a genuine hawkish repricing, the front end should have moved with the back end, not against it. A 30-year yield spike alongside a falling 2-year is the textbook signature of a term-premium or growth-optimism repricing, not tightening fear; Warsh's own explanation ("market participants are learning to play the ball and not the referee") is an admission that the Fed deliberately withheld guidance rather than demonstrated new resolve. The three dissenting presidents — Hammack, Kashkari, Logan — are the same voices who have pushed for tightening since spring; their dissent reflects a persistent minority position, not a shift in the committee's center of gravity, which held 9-3 in the same direction as June. The policy rate, in other words, is exactly where it was a meeting ago — the "hawkish hold" narrative is doing more work than the actual vote or the front-end yield move supports.

## 12. One To Watch
