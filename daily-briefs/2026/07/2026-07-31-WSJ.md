**China-Panama retaliation is the clearest live case study of tariff/trade-policy blowback with market mechanics attached.** After Panama's Supreme Court (under U.S. pressure) ousted CK Hutchison from the Balboa and Cristóbal canal ports, China began systematically detaining Panama-flagged vessels — 500 detentions since January, 146 in May alone (versus 23 in January), concentrated on a fleet that carries a "meaningful share" of U.S.-bound trade through the Canal.
