**[WEAK SIGNAL] The renminbi's reserve-currency ambitions are quietly stalling on the hardest of three separate climbs.** China has made real progress on trade settlement and cross-border lending in its own currency, but the "investment" leg — foreigners willingly holding renminbi assets rather than merely using them — remains stuck: onshore bond holdings by foreigners are under 2% of the total and falling, central-bank renminbi reserves are flat around 2%.
