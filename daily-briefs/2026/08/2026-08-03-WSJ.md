## 1. Today's Themes

**AI capital expenditure is bifurcating into a two-tier market, and the tier line is "do you rent out a cloud."**
Amazon (AWS +37% revenue, 39% operating margin), Microsoft (Azure +43% revenue) and Alphabet (cloud +82% revenue) were rewarded with the largest one-day market-cap gains in history — Microsoft alone added roughly $450 billion in a single session, and Microsoft plus Amazon added a combined ~$950 billion since reporting. The distinguishing variable investors are now pricing is not AI spend itself but whether that spend generates an external, contracted revenue stream (AWS's 10-year, $100+ billion Anthropic contract is the template) versus internal cost absorption.

Warsh is also weighing fewer scheduled policy meetings.

OPEC+ raised output for a sixth straight month regardless.

NYC's pied-à-terre tax rollout (960,000 properties flagged for a levy meant to hit ~10,000) is the live case study in messy wealth-tax administration.

**[WEAK SIGNAL] AI is quietly dissolving the employer-employee unit while raising fiduciary-liability questions no one has priced.**

Together these describe AI absorbing both the labor and liability function of a company.
