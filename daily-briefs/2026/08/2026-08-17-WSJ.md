## 1. Today's Themes

**The Fed is deliberately dismantling its own credibility infrastructure, not building it.** Chairman Kevin Warsh has cut FOMC post-meeting statement word counts by 49% year-over-year, trimmed minutes by 24%, cut governor speeches by 27%, and gone dot-free at the SEP since taking the chair in June. The most egregious miss — promising a zero funds rate out to year-end 2023 as late as March 2021 — directly fed Silicon Valley Bank's insolvency (SVB bought long-duration Treasurys on the strength of that guidance) and left the Fed defending "transitory" inflation language into December 2021, months after unemployment had fallen to 3.9% and inflation was running at 6.1%.
