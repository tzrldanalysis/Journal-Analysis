## 7. What Would Have To Be True

**Implication:** Advanced economies can manage a "$2tn interest bill" without abrupt fiscal retrenchment or a disorderly debt event.

**Assumption:** Growth and tax revenue must outpace the compounding effect of higher-for-longer real rates on sovereign rollover costs, and governments must retain market access on acceptable terms.

**Fragility:** Any further leg up in yields, or a loss of investor confidence in fiscal discipline — the Aston Martin-HPS dispute shows credit funds are already willing to litigate aggressively over terms — would force the "difficult trade-offs" the Big Read describes into an immediate, disorderly choice rather than a managed one.

**Implication:** Frontier AI labs like Anthropic can commercialise via public listing while simultaneously satisfying safety and governance scrutiny well enough to avoid a regulatory or reputational setback.

**Assumption:** Anthropic's bioweapon-risk mitigation research must be seen as credible and sufficient by regulators, banks, and counterparties (UBS is already treating AI fluency as a baseline hiring requirement) through the IPO process, not exposed as inadequate after listing.

**Fragility:** Any publicised failure of the safeguards — a jailbreak or bioweapon-adjacent misuse surfacing — between now and listing would collide the commercialisation and safety narratives at the worst possible moment for a public offering.
