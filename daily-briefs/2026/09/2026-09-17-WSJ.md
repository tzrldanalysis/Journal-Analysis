## 3. Geopolitics & Policy

**Trade, tariffs and sanctions.** The House passed legislation 262-159 (Senate-approved last month, heading to Trump's desk) authorizing tariffs up to 100% on the five biggest importers of Russian oil and gas and extending Iran sanctions to 2031. China accounts for 51% of Russia's fossil-fuel export revenue (Sen. Jeanne Shaheen), making it the bill's de facto primary target even though it isn't named as such in today's coverage. Separately, Trump threatened "very serious tariffs" against the EU, or to "stop trading with Europe on many things," if Brussels proceeds with granting Canada associate-member status — layered on top of existing US tariffs on Canadian dairy, alcohol and motorcycles, and a partial rollback/re-imposition shuffle on the existing 50% tariff regime (cement and road salt exempted; ATVs, boats and cheeses added) after US-Canada trade talks collapsed in late August.

**Institutional and structural dynamics.** Von der Leyen's Strasbourg speech proposing Canada as the EU's first "associate member" — with discussions reportedly covering defense supply-chain integration, joint cloud/data infrastructure, AI cooperation and partial single-market access without full membership obligations — is a genuine structural first: it tests whether the EU can build a tier of partial belonging that doesn't invite exploitation by other partial partners (Norway, Switzerland, post-Brexit UK all have their own bespoke arrangements to compare against). Canada's own resistance to full free-movement and budget-contribution terms shows the limits of how far this goes. Separately, an international tribunal in The Hague convicted former Kosovo president Hashim Thaci of war crimes (25-year sentence) — a reminder of the accountability infrastructure operating in the background of today's more contested war-crimes conversation around Gaza. The US and Belarus also advanced an incremental normalization track: 25 prisoners released in exchange for partial sanctions relief on two Belarusian companies, with more relief contingent on further releases.
