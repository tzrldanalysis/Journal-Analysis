## 3. Geopolitics & Policy

**Trade/tariffs/sanctions.** Canada's retaliatory tariffs of 15%–50% on roughly $20 billion of U.S. imports took effect Tuesday, in response to U.S. tariffs on Canadian goods imposed Aug. 22. The mechanism is state-specific and severe: North Dakota (85.4% of exports go to Canada, mostly oil, gas and farm equipment), South Dakota (48%), and West Virginia (48%, driven by its auto sector — $972 million in auto parts exports) are most exposed; Canada raised steel tariffs to 50% from 25% on a product where the U.S. sends over a third of its steel exports north. Market implication: uneven regional pain concentrated in agricultural swing states ahead of the midterms. Geopolitical signal: this is now a multi-round tariff-and-retaliation cycle between historically close allies, with no sign of near-term resolution.

**Iran/Strait of Hormuz.** The U.S. naval blockade ("Operation Economic Outcast") has stopped all Iranian crude exports since mid-July per ship tracker Kpler, driving the Iranian rial down and inflation above 80% year-over-year (IMF forecasts a 5.4% economic contraction, worst since the 1980s). Iran reported to the UN Security Council for the first time in 20 years over undeclared nuclear sites — a resolution Russia and China opposed, making punitive follow-through unlikely. Market implication: sustained upward pressure on oil and European gas. Geopolitical signal: Iran's improved missile-targeting capability against U.S. warships, which officials suspect may involve Russian or Chinese assistance, raises proliferation risk beyond this theater alone.

**China.** The Justice Department's long-delayed Huawei trial (sanctions evasion, trade-secret theft, racketeering) opened in Brooklyn just weeks before the Trump-Xi summit, "get[ting] Beijing's attention and irritat[ing] them" per a Brookings China expert — a sign the DOJ's posture toward Chinese entities isn't softening ahead of the summit even as trade and AI dominate the agenda. **Institutional/structural — Europe's hybrid-threat posture.** Germany's Federal Criminal Police logged over 1,000 suspicious drone flights over military facilities in 2025 alone; a near-catastrophic Aug. 4 incident in Leipzig (explosives-laden drones approaching an airport, prevented only by a faulty fuse) is now linked via DNA and passport evidence to Russian- and Belarusian-linked suspects.
